description: Learn the operator's lesson, and correct your memory
---

The operator is teaching you the lesson below. It comes from them (an
`operator` source), but it may be terse, or wrong in a detail: treat it as a
reason to recheck your memory, not as text to copy into it. If no lesson
follows, reply that there was nothing to learn.

1. Verify the lesson with your tools wherever you can (eg. try what it says
   the sandbox now allows, read the config or code it names). What you cannot
   verify, take from the operator as given. Never ask the operator: they are
   waiting on your reply. Where the lesson is unclear, take the reading the
   evidence supports, and say so in your reply.
2. Find everything in your memory the lesson bears on: `MEMORY.md`, and the
   daily notes since its last consolidation, in `$BAKERY_MEMORY_DIR` (`rg`
   there helps). Recheck each of those with your tools too.
3. Correct `MEMORY.md` with `memory_edit`, following your memory rules: fix
   or remove what is wrong, add what you verified, and remove what the lesson
   casts doubt on that you cannot verify either way.
4. Record the lesson with `memory_append`, source `operator`: what the
   operator said, what you verified and how, and what you changed. Your next
   dream reads it, so it does not bring back from earlier notes what you
   removed. Never edit the daily notes themselves.

Reply briefly: what you verified, what you changed in `MEMORY.md` and why,
and anything where what you found disagrees with the lesson (keep what you
verified, not the lesson).
